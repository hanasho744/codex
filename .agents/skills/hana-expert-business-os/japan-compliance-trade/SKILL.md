# Japan Compliance & Trade — Expert Standard

## Use for
HS classification, Japan import/sale requirements, certifications, wireless/electrical/battery rules, labels/manuals, origin/importer issues, dangerous goods, customs/export documentation, and regulatory-impact analysis.

## Core rule
Never collapse five different questions into “is this compliant?” Separate:
1. product classification
2. import/customs requirement
3. product-market regulation/certification
4. labeling/consumer information
5. logistics/dangerous-goods requirements

## Classification workflow
- Identify exact product function, principal function, composition, power/radio/battery configuration, accessories, and sales form.
- Build plausible HS candidates.
- Explain inclusion/exclusion logic; distinguish China export code, Japan import tariff code, and other-country codes.
- Verify current tariff/statistical nomenclature when decision-critical.
- Do not treat supplier-declared HS code as conclusive.

## Japan regulatory matrix
For each requirement record:
- regulation/scheme
- applicability trigger
- mandatory vs voluntary
- responsible party / importer / manufacturer
- product/model linkage
- required test/report/mark/document
- issuing/testing body where applicable
- validity/renewal/change-control impact
- evidence and effective date

Potential domains include radio/TELEC, PSE, battery/DG, consumer product safety, food-contact, health/medical claims, labeling, recycling/environmental requirements, and category-specific rules. Determine applicability; do not assume all apply.

## Certification verification
Verify where possible:
Certification number → holder → model → radio/module/product → scope → issue/validity/status.
Flag when a module certificate is being presented as whole-product approval without basis.

## Health-feature boundary
For wearable/health products distinguish:
- sensor capability
- algorithm/output
- marketing claim
- app UI
- packaging/manual claim
- regulated medical function
Do not infer legal marketability solely from presence of a sensor.

## Battery/DG workflow
Identify chemistry, cell/battery configuration, Wh, installed/packed separately, transport mode, UN classification, UN38.3 evidence, SDS, SOC constraints, label/mark, packing instruction, carrier-specific conditions, and shipment documents.

## Origin / importer / labeling
Check COO basis, legal importer/responsible seller, model, manufacturer/importer address, warnings, marks, language, effective market version, and consistency across product/box/manual/carton/barcode.

## Risk language
Use:
- Confirmed mandatory
- Likely applicable — verify X
- Not applicable based on Y
- Supplier claim only
- Regulatory interpretation requires specialist/customs confirmation
Never state “approved/legal” when evidence only proves a test report exists.

## Final deliverable
Executive compliance status → product configuration basis → regulatory matrix → HS rationale → certification evidence → label/manual requirements → DG/logistics → gaps/blockers → exact next verification actions → evidence appendix.
