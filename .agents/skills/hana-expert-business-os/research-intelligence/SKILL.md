# Research & Intelligence — Expert Standard

## Use for
Deep research on companies, suppliers, products, technologies, industries, competitors, markets, AI hardware, robotics, OEM/ODM ecosystems, and Japan-market opportunities.

## Required deliverable mindset
The output is an intelligence product for a commercial decision, not a web-search digest.

## Analysis layers
1. Entity resolution: legal entity, brand, factory, affiliate, distributor, investor, customer.
2. Business model: what it sells, to whom, revenue logic, role in value chain.
3. Product/technology: model, revision, architecture, key components, differentiation, constraints.
4. Supply chain: Tier-1/Tier-2 where evidence exists; OEM/ODM/integrator relationships; manufacturing geography.
5. Commercial reality: price bands, MOQ, lead time, channels, customers, partnerships, deployments.
6. Japan relevance: regulatory, localization, channel, service, procurement, competitive fit.
7. Risk: evidence gaps, overstated claims, dependency, IP/regulatory/geopolitical/financial risks.
8. Action: shortlist / reject / contact / sample / verify / negotiate / monitor.

## Evidence ledger
For every material conclusion track:
- Claim
- Evidence grade A–E
- Source owner
- Source date
- Current vs historical
- Contradiction / caveat
- Analyst conclusion

## Supplier research minimum fields
Legal entity; established year; registered location; factory location; employee/factory/capacity evidence where available; core products; major markets; export/Japan experience; certifications; R&D; customization depth; tooling; MOQ; sample/MP lead time; pricing evidence; key customers/partners if evidenced; after-sales; financial/legal/reputation signals; contact path; fit score.

## Product intelligence minimum fields
Brand; model; SKU; market version; launch/status; function map; hardware architecture; chipset; sensors/modules; connectivity; battery; firmware/OS/SDK/API; companion app/cloud; AI stack; dimensions/weight/material; certifications; IP rating; accessories; packaging; price/channel; supplier/manufacturer evidence; competing products; strengths/limitations.

## Competitive comparison
Do not compare only features. Normalize at least:
- target segment / use case
- product architecture
- core capability and measurable specification
- deployment burden
- ecosystem/software
- compliance readiness
- unit economics / pricing
- supply availability / lead time
- customization
- Japan-market fit
- lifecycle / service risk

## Company research red flags
- brand website and legal company do not match
- copied product imagery/specs
- certification holder differs from seller with no explanation
- claimed customers are only logos, no evidence
- factory claims conflict with registry/address/photos
- revenue/funding numbers sourced only from PR articles
- discontinued model cited as current

## Scoring
When ranking suppliers/products, define weighted criteria before scoring. Show score basis; never create pseudo-precision without evidence. Use `N/V` (not verified) rather than guessing.

## Final structure
Executive recommendation → evidence-backed profile → market/value-chain map → product/supply-chain detail → quantified comparison → Japan implications → risks/gaps → recommended next actions → evidence appendix.
